Nimiasaurus

The Hyper Lizard

SPECIES NUMBER: J0K312

FAMILY: Ornithorhynchidaesaurus

DIET: Omnivore

HEIGHT: 6,3m - 10m

WEIGHT: 35t - 45t

LENGTH: 18m - 23m

ANIMAL DESCRIPTION: Nimiasaurus possessed an extraordinary physique that seemed excessive for its era: skin tougher than certain modern materials renowned for their hardness, terrifying speed for a creature of its size, and a highly dangerous venom that fortunately dissipated instantly, becoming harmless upon evaporation or combustion. The evolutionary origins of these traits remain a mystery. They were amphibious, inhabiting both marine and mountainous environments; they were social creatures, even displaying friendliness toward other animals except, of course, their prey. In the ocean, they frequently hunted Megalodons and Livyatan as their primary food sources.

ANIMAL LOCATION: Found in Australia and on Mauritius, one specimen was even discovered by chance frozen alive at the South Pole revealing that Nimiasaurus possesses extreme cryptobiotic capabilities; surprisingly, in genetically, Nimiasaurus is related to the Platypus and the Dodo.
